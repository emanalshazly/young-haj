# Content Review Policy

Young Haj separates deterministic software validation from religious and educational content review.

An item may move from `pending` to `reviewed` only when the ledger records the reviewer, reviewer role, UTC review date, content hash, reviewed scope, findings, and disposition. Automated tests can validate structure and gameplay; they cannot certify religious accuracy, age appropriateness, or learning outcomes.

Current release status: **BLOCKED for domain-review claims** until review receipts are committed. The application must not describe pending content as approved, certified, or expert-reviewed.
